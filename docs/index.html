01 · Motivation

From rigid turn-taking to natural full-duplex conversation.

The page should quickly explain why full-duplex SLMs matter, where existing paradigms fail, and why Lychee-FD is positioned as an efficiency-intelligence bridge.

A

Half-duplex feels unnatural

Traditional systems strictly alternate between listening and speaking, which breaks the rhythm of human conversation.

B

Native sharing causes interference

Fully shared speech-text models are efficient, but deep-layer objectives can fight each other and degrade knowledge retention.

C

Thinker-Talker adds latency

Decoupling modules can protect semantic ability, but separate generation paths often increase inference cost and engineering complexity.

02 · Core Insight

Two optimization symptoms motivate the design.

These cards are intentionally modular: later you can replace each SVG mock chart with the final paper figure, animation, or interactive Plotly panel.

Gradient direction conflict

Shallow layers can share representations, but deeper layers show increasingly orthogonal or negative optimization directions between semantic and acoustic objectives.

Design response: split deep heads →

Semantic supervision dilution

Padding sparse text tokens into dense audio frames weakens the semantic learning signal, letting acoustic reconstruction dominate the optimization process.

Design response: semantic channel →
